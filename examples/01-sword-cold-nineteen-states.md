# Case Study: "One Sword Chills Nineteen Provinces" (剑寒十九州)

> A 15-second duel inspired by: 剑气纵横三万里，一剑光寒十九州
> Generated with: Seedance 2.5, 720p, 9:16, 300 credits
> VLM Quality Score: 9/10

## Concept

Two master swordsmen meet on a frozen plain. One strike decides everything.

**The poetic challenge**: How do you visualize "30,000 li of sword energy" and "19 provinces chilled by one sword"?

**Solution**: Scale through environment reaction, not explosion size:
- "30,000 li" → Ground fissure extending to the horizon
- "19 provinces" → Mountains collapsing one by one in silence
