# Case Study: "Moonlit Twins" (月下双生)

> A 27-second identity-reveal duel (3 segments: 11s + 10s + 6s)
> The sequel hook: the villain has the hero's face

## Story

After the climactic battle, the hero discovers his nemesis bears his own face. A flashback reveals they trained together 500 years ago. The final strike is not against an enemy — it's against himself.

## Key Technique: Same-Face Casting

Both characters share identical 固有资产 except for ONE difference:
```
剑主: black pupils
夜无极: dark red pupils
```
This single-palette-difference creates the "mirror self" reveal without needing two different character designs.

## Prompt Structure (3 Segments)

### Segment A (11s) — Confrontation + Flashback
- Moonlit pavilion, two identical men face each other
- Warm golden flashback: two boys training together
- Return to cold present

### Segment B (10s) — The Strike
- Both draw simultaneously
- One flash of sword light → moon splits in half
- A snowflake falls, cut in two by unseen force

### Segment C (6s) — The Reveal
- Villain's sword cracks, he smiles
- Split screen: black pupils (left) vs red pupils (right)
- Black screen
